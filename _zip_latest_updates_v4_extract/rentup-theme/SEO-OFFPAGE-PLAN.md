# SEO-OFFPAGE-PLAN.md

**Project:** Murailles Immobilier — https://murailles-immobilier.com/
**Market:** Real estate, Marrakech & Morocco (FR primary, EN secondary)
**Date:** 2026-06-14

> **Ethics rule (non-negotiable):** No purchased links, no PBNs, no fake
> backlinks, no link exchanges at scale, no hidden links. Every tactic below
> earns links through real listings, real relationships, and real content.

---

## 1. Recommended anchor-text mix

Keep anchors **natural and varied** (over-optimised exact-match anchors trigger
spam filters). Target distribution across all earned links:

| Anchor type | Share | Examples |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| Brand | ~40% | `Murailles Immobilier`, `Agence Murailles` |
| Naked URL | ~20% | `murailles-immobilier.com` |
| Partial / topical | ~25% | `agence immobilière à Marrakech`, `immobilier de luxe Marrakech` |
| Exact-match (sparingly) | ~10% | `riads à vendre Marrakech`, `villas à vendre Marrakech` |
| Generic | ~5% | `voir l'agence`, `en savoir plus` |

---

## 2. Local citation strategy (highest ROI first)

NAP (Name, Address, Phone) must be **byte-identical** everywhere.

1. **Google Business Profile** — primary. Category: *Agence immobilière*. Add
   service area (Marrakech, Palmeraie, Guéliz, Médina), photos, posts, Q&A,
   and respond to every review. This is the single biggest local-SEO lever.
2. **Bing Places** + **Apple Business Connect**.
3. Moroccan directories:
   - Avito.ma, Mubawab.ma, Sarouty.ma (property portals — also lead sources)
   - Telecontact.ma, Annuaire.ma, Pages Jaunes Maroc
   - Le Matin / L'Économiste business listings
4. International real-estate portals (luxury segment): Green-Acres,
   JamesEdition, Properstar, Idealista International.
5. **Consistency audit:** quarterly check that name, address, phone, and
   `https://murailles-immobilier.com` match across all of the above.

## 3. Backlink acquisition (white-hat)

### Marrakech / Moroccan real estate
- Guest articles on Moroccan property & lifestyle blogs (market trends,
  "acheter un riad dans la médina" buyer guides).
- Partner pages: notaries, architects, riad-renovation firms, interior
  designers — reciprocal *referrals*, not link schemes.

### Local media & PR
- Press releases for milestones (new luxury listings, agency anniversary,
  market reports) to Le360, Hespress, Médias24, TelQuel, local Marrakech press.
- Offer expert commentary to journalists covering Moroccan real estate
  (HARO-style outreach) → earns editorial, high-authority links.

### Business associations & directories
- Chambre Immobilière régionale, FNPI (Fédération Nationale des Promoteurs
  Immobiliers) membership pages.
- Chamber of Commerce Marrakech-Safi listing.
- Expat communities (Marrakech expat forums, relocation guides) — strong for
  the EN audience buying second homes.

### Property portals (links + leads)
- Mubawab, Avito, Sarouty agency profiles linking back to the site.

## 4. Content that earns links (on-page → off-page flywheel)

Already supported by the theme's blog + property CPT:
- **Neighbourhood guides:** Guéliz, Palmeraie, Médina, Hivernage — "où acheter à Marrakech".
- **Market reports:** quarterly price trends (data assets attract citations).
- **Buyer guides (EN):** "Buying property in Morocco as a foreigner" — links
  from expat/relocation sites.

Promote each via newsletter (footer signup already live) + GBP posts + social.
